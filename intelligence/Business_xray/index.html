Trigger Conditions

For the point at which the official explanation is no longer sufficient.

Business X-Ray™ is designed for a bounded but consequential question—where acting on an incomplete explanation could produce expensive, disruptive or difficult-to-reverse decisions.

  • Growth has reduced visibility across functions, entities or leadership levels.
  • Different people provide materially different explanations for the same recurring outcome.
  • Decisions repeatedly stall, return for approval or depend on one individual.
  • Responsibilities appear clear formally but operate differently in practice.
  • Important information is fragmented across people, systems and informal channels.
  • A restructure, system change, leadership intervention or investment decision depends on understanding the present condition first.
What KINGLIS examines

Evidence before explanation.

KINGLIS examines the parts of the organisation that materially affect the agreed question. Depending on the evidence, this may include strategy, structure, governance, operations, leadership and performance.

What the engagement seeks to establish

  • What can be directly observed from the available evidence.
  • Which explanations are supported, contradicted or remain untested.
  • Which structural conditions are producing or sustaining the outcome.
  • What risks and dependencies follow from those conditions.
  • Which matters require priority executive attention.
Decisions

Where decisions originate, who can authorise them, where they stall and how completion is evidenced.

Authority

The difference between formal responsibility and authority exercised in practice.

Information

Where material information originates, how it moves, where it fragments and who can access it.

Workflows

The handoffs, dependencies, controls and exceptions shaping execution.

Knowledge

Where critical organisational knowledge is documented, concentrated or vulnerable.

Conditions

The structures and recurring mechanisms that make observed outcomes more likely.

Doctrine: Understanding precedes change: Evidence → Intelligence → Advisory.

What the client receives

Four outputs. Each derived from the same evidential record.

01 — Executive Findings Report

A focused, evidence-grounded account of the question, sources examined, material limitations, organisational reality, defensible chronology, findings, structural conditions, risks, implications, priorities and open questions.

02 — Structural Intelligence Map

A one-page visual showing the authority, decision pathways, information movement, dependencies, bottlenecks, control gaps and reinforcing conditions materially connected to the question.

03 — 90-Day Action Architecture

A sequenced architecture connecting each priority to the condition being addressed, accountable owner, required action, dependency, evidence of completion and 30-, 60- or 90-day horizon. It is not an implementation plan.

04 — Executive Findings Meeting

One 60-minute confidential meeting to present the findings, explain evidential confidence, clarify implications and establish the available decision landscape.

Corrections: One consolidated factual-correction round is included for demonstrable factual inaccuracies. It does not include new evidence, expanded analysis, restructuring or removal of supported findings.

Evidence Sufficiency

Delivery begins only when sufficiency is confirmed in writing.

The delivery clock begins only when KINGLIS confirms Evidence Sufficiency in writing. Sufficiency means that required documents have been received or their absence recorded, material is usable, the question remains within scope, interviews are scheduled, restrictions are known and the evidence can responsibly support analysis.

KINGLIS—not the client—determines Evidence Sufficiency. Client-caused delays, cancellations, access failures or material evidence submitted afterwards may pause or revise delivery.

FAQ

Clarity before engagement.

Is this disruptive to the organisation?

The engagement is designed to minimise unnecessary disruption. It uses existing evidence and no more than three targeted stakeholder interviews. No employee-wide survey, workshop or all-hands process is included.

When does the ten-business-day period begin?

Only after KINGLIS confirms Evidence Sufficiency in writing. Payment, onboarding or partial document receipt does not start the delivery clock.

Will KINGLIS tell us what to do?

KINGLIS first establishes what the evidence supports and the conditions producing the observed outcomes. The 90-Day Action Architecture then sequences evidence-grounded priorities. Implementation remains a separate decision and engagement.

What if the evidence does not support a firm conclusion?

KINGLIS will state the limitation directly, distinguish supported findings from indicative conditions and identify the evidence or clarification required. A report is not manufactured to create false certainty.

Can the scope expand after commencement?

Yes, but only through written variation or a separate engagement. Additional evidence, interviews, entities, countries, functions or questions are not absorbed silently into Foundation.

What happens after delivery?

The engagement closes after the factual-correction process and Executive Findings Meeting. Implementation support or continuing Organisational Intelligence Advisory may be proposed separately where appropriate.
