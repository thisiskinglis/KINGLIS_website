Trigger Conditions

For when something isn't working as it should — and the reason isn't clear.

This work is designed for business problems where the visible symptoms don't fully explain what's happening, and making changes before understanding the cause could create further problems.

  • The same problem keeps returning despite attempts to fix it.
  • Different people have different explanations for what's going wrong.
  • Decisions stall, circle back or repeatedly depend on one person.
  • Responsibilities look clear on paper but work differently in practice.
  • Important information is scattered across people, systems or informal channels.
  • Growth or change has created problems that are difficult to trace to a single cause.
  • A significant decision or change needs to be made, but you're not confident you understand the current situation well enough yet.
What KINGLIS examines

Evidence before explanation.

KINGLIS examines only those parts of the organization materially connected to the agreed question. This may include strategy, structure, governance, operations, leadership and performance.

What the engagement seeks to establish

  • What can be directly observed from the available evidence.
  • Which explanations are supported, contradicted or remain untested.
  • Which structural conditions are producing or sustaining the outcome.
  • What risks and dependencies follow from those conditions.
  • Which matters require priority executive attention.
Decisions

Where decisions originate, who can authorise them, where they stall and how completion is evidenced.

Authority

The difference between formal responsibility and authority exercised in practice.

Information

Where material information originates, how it moves, where it fragments and who can access it.

Workflows

The handoffs, dependencies, controls and exceptions shaping execution.

Knowledge

Where critical organizational knowledge is documented, concentrated or vulnerable.

Conditions

The structures and recurring mechanisms that make observed outcomes more likely.

What you receive

Four practical outputs from the investigation.

01 - Executive Findings Report

A clear account of what's happening, what's causing it, what the evidence supports, and what needs attention. It includes the key findings, risks, implications, priorities and any important questions that remain unresolved.

02 - Structural Map

A visual showing how the problem fits together — connecting the underlying conditions, decisions, dependencies and constraints to the outcomes you're seeing.

03 - 90-Day Action Architecture

A practical 30-, 60- and 90-day sequence showing what needs to happen, in what order, who needs to own it, and what should deliberately not change yet.

04 - Executive Findings Meeting

A 60-minute confidential meeting to walk through the findings, answer questions and discuss what the diagnosis means for the decisions ahead.

FAQ

Clarity before engagement.

Is this disruptive to the organization?

Designed to minimise disruption. Uses existing evidence and no more than three targeted stakeholder interviews.

When does the ten-business-day period begin?

Only after KINGLIS confirms Evidence Sufficiency in writing.

Will KINGLIS tell us what to do?

KINGLIS first establishes what the evidence supports, what requires priority executive attention and what should deliberately not change yet. The 90-Day Action Architecture™ then translates those findings into a controlled sequence for leadership.

What if the evidence does not support a firm conclusion?

KINGLIS will state the limitation directly. Uncertainty, contradiction and evidential limits are reported rather than converted into unsupported conclusions.

Can the scope expand after commencement?

Yes, but only through written variation or a separate engagement.

What happens after delivery?

The engagement closes after the factual-correction process and Executive Findings Meeting.
