Confidential Intake™

Required before any KINGLIS engagement.

Every KINGLIS engagement begins with a Confidential Intake. Before findings, investigative work or advisory can begin, we establish an initial understanding of the organisation, the circumstances that prompted the enquiry, and the decisions that depend upon achieving greater clarity.

This is not an assessment or consultation. It enables KINGLIS to determine whether the matter falls within our field of work and whether a Business X-Ray™ or another engagement is appropriate.

What happens next

Your submission will receive a Private Reference. KINGLIS will review the circumstances, the question requiring resolution and the available evidence. You will then receive one of the following:

  • • A request for limited clarification
  • • An invitation to a confidential scoping conversation
  • • A recommended engagement pathway
  • • Confirmation that the matter falls outside the appropriate scope of KINGLIS

Submitting an intake does not create an advisory relationship or obligation for either party.
