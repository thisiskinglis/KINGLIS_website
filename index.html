FOUNDER

KAYLEE-JANE INGLIS

Kaylee-Jane works confidentially with founders, executives and organizations navigating complex operational, governance and strategic challenges — where the stakes are high, the picture is unclear, and the cost of getting it wrong is significant.

She founded KINGLIS to establish Organizational Intelligence as a practical discipline for executive decision-making. Her work examines organizational architecture, governance, operating models and evidence-led systems inquiry — connecting fragmented evidence, surfacing the conditions that shape outcomes, and translating complexity into decision-ready intelligence.

Her practice is built on a single conviction: that most organizational problems are not solved by more advice. They are solved by better understanding. She has a rare ability to connect fragmented evidence, surface the conditions that shape outcomes, and reason through complexity that resists conventional analysis—translating what is difficult to see into intelligence that is impossible to ignore.
