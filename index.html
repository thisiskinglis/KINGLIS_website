What is organizational intelligence?

Organizational intelligence is the ability to arrive at organizational truth before you act. Not dashboards. The conditions that actually drive performance.

How do you diagnose organizational problems?

Establish what actually happened from evidence. Then understand what it means. Then design what comes next. Evidence before assumption.

01

Why do transformation programmes fail?

They act on narrative rather than evidence. They address symptoms rather than the conditions that produce them. The organization changes. The underlying problem does not.

02
02

What is the difference between a diagnostic and an audit?

An audit verifies compliance against a known standard. A diagnostic reconstructs reality when the record is fragmented, disputed, or incomplete – when what actually happened is not yet established. Most organizations need a diagnostic. They commission an audit.

03
03

Why can't we do this ourselves?

The understanding that governs an organization's decisions also governs how the organization perceives itself. An organization cannot assess the accuracy of its own self-understanding using the same understanding whose accuracy is in question. This is not a failure of intelligence. It is a structural property of self-referential systems.

04
04

What does organizational intelligence actually produce?

A defensible account of what the organization actually is – its real capabilities, its operative values, its actual decision-making patterns – against which the accuracy of the existing organizational understanding can be assessed. And the intelligence to act on that account.

Who is this for?

Founders who sense that the organization has become something they no longer fully understand. Executives making major decisions who are not confident that their understanding of the organization is accurate. Boards and investors who need an independent account of organizational reality that does not depend on management's own assessment.

No pitch deck. No diagnostic framework to download. The work begins with what is true.
