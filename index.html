KINGLIS Schools — Pilot Term 1

Find out what helps the child regulate.

Teachers in Ballito report the same daily: anxious learners, big feelings, difficulty naming emotions, low resilience. This programme gives them a toolkit they can use in class — not a diagnosis.

Benefit 01
Emotional literacy Builds vocabulary for feelings, recognises body signals and emotional cues without pressure to talk.
Benefit 02
Anxiety & regulation Calm, predictable hands-on making — 5-finger breathing, pause button, sensory squish/smooth/press.
Benefit 03
Resilience & confidence Strength Shields, Bridge Builders, Resilience Pathways — focus on what they can do, not what is wrong.
Ultimate 6-Week Journey — SEN-informed
Safety → Feelings → Calm Kit → Safe Place → Strengths → Resilience.
W1: My Safe Hands — safe textures, 5-finger breathing
W2: Feelings Have Shapes — Clay Emotion Symbols
W3: My Calm Kit starts — pinch pot / box for coping tools
W4: Safe Place Sculptures + Support Network figures
W5: Strength Shields + Bridge Builders — worry to calm
W6: Resilience Pathways + Next Steps token — celebration

Min 6, max 8. Visual schedules, choice boards, optional gloves/tools, permission to observe. Sharing optional. Artwork never diagnostic.
• Not diagnosis • Not psychotherapy • Not HPCSA arts therapy • Materials supplied — air-dry clay, non-toxic
Classroom impact: calmer transitions, better use of coping language, increased participation when learners have a regulation toolkit.
